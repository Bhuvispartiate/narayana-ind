## 2. What the Company Does

Narayana Industries describes itself as a **precision engineering and manufacturing company** specializing in:
- Automotive and railway **air spring metal components**
- **Heavy machining**
- **Structural fabrication**
- **Line production for automobile parts**

It also states involvement in **project-based works through tender contracts**, positioning itself as a partner for **railway, automobile, and industrial manufacturing tenders**.
